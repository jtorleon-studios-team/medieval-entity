## Intro :

Introduce **hostile medieval knights** to your Minecraft world with the *Medieval Entity* mod! This mod adds a unique twist to your game by introducing **different types of knights** that spawn in various biomes. These knights will provide an exciting challenge for any adventurer brave enough to face them!

## Features:
- Adds **hostile knights** in four different biomes:
  - **Knight Swamp** – Mysterious and dangerous knights lurking in the swamps.
  - **Knight Desert** – Fierce knights that dwell in the harsh desert environment.
  - **Knight Hills** – Towering knights found in the rocky hills, ready for battle.
  - **Knight Forest** – Stealthy knights that emerge from the forest to challenge you.
- A **new challenge** for players seeking to battle strong, medieval-inspired enemies.

**Perfect for**: Players looking to add a new level of difficulty and adventure to their Minecraft world, ideal for **PvE gameplay**, **map creators**, and fans of **medieval themes**.
